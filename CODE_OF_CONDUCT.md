# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of experience, education, identity, disability, appearance,
nationality, ethnicity, religion, or other personal characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Make room for newcomers and different research traditions.
- Disclose uncertainty, limitations, and conflicts of interest.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property, embargoes, and unpublished work.
- Accept maintainer decisions about safety, scope, and evidence standards.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person’s private information without permission.
- Uploading credentials, private manuscripts, restricted datasets, or identifiable
  user records.
- Fabricating citations, benchmarks, results, identities, or adoption claims.
- Encouraging quota evasion, access-control bypass, or unsafe execution of
  untrusted repositories.
- Repeated disruption after a scope or moderation decision has been explained.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when behavior is unsafe or incompatible with this code.
Responses should be proportionate and should preserve private information.

Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer channel when one is listed
on the repository. If no private channel is available, open a neutral issue
requesting private contact and do not include names, credentials, personal data,
or sensitive evidence in the public issue.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when a person officially represents the
project in other public or private spaces.
